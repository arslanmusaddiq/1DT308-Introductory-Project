# Assignment: The professional role of an engineer

This assignment is intended to provide a deeper understanding of the professional role of an engineer in the technology sector. Students will explore and analyze work environments, work tasks, and corporate cultures by gathering information from companies and professionals. The end result is an oral presentation based on these insights. You will either interview or contact a company, or you will take information from the guest lecturer.

You will present your report at the project presentation or during oral exam. **You individually have a maximum of 3 minutes to do this per person.**


## Presentation 

You will present orally, and display your report submission in the project repository as a markdown file. Not a power point presentation.
Present your insights and reflections in an oral presentation (3 minutes).

•	Focus on effectively communicating your main points.
•	Focus on your personal reflection.

## Report - content

At least 3 companies, 1-2 pages reflection report, max 1000 words.
Your reflection report should contain the following answers with reflection for at *least three companies*.

•	Description of the companies. What do they do?
•	Business idea and product?
•	What would a working day look like for a developer, and what are the typical tasks?
•	How do they work in projects, and what project methods are used?
•	What tools do they use?

The reflection report should be uploaded as a markdown file in the same repository as your project. The report is individual, name it after your name.
